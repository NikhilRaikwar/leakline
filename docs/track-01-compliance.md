## Runtime example and bounded claim

The saved live investigation is tied to `google-research-datasets/go_emotions`, config `simplified`, revision `add492243ff905527e67aeb8b80c082af02207c3`. It reports 1,500 inspected examples each in train/validation/test; source split sizes 43,410/5,426/5,427; 7 confirmed edges; 3 crossing components (2 train/test and 1 train/validation). Policy A quarantined two training rows for the test-preserving boundary, independently verified the analyzed graph, and explicitly left the train/validation group. The plan was not approved by the model. This is a partial sample and a text-duplicate analysis, not a claim of full-corpus or semantic-leakage absence.

Synthetic integration fixtures demonstrate multi-hop and failure behavior only; they are not described as GoEmotions observations. The baseline comparison is one paired run, and the restricted baseline had no source facts. Its abstention is a safety outcome, not evidence of lower general model quality.
